## Selected work

> The projects below are **unofficial concept redesigns** — self-directed work, not commissioned by or affiliated with the businesses named. Built to show an approach.

### ☕ Coffee Matters — concept redesign
A redesign of a St. John's and Paradise coffee roaster, built around the two things the business actually needs: in-store visits and catering inquiries. Warm Newfoundland artisan direction, editorial typography, an interactive menu with dietary filters, a qualified catering funnel, and live "open now" hours in local time.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind v4` · `GSAP ScrollTrigger` · `Lenis`

Design tokens drive the palette, content lives as typed data ready to move into a CMS, and every animation has a reduced-motion fallback.

### 🍕 Venice Pizzeria · 🦪 Adelaide Oyster · 🥯 Bagel Café · 🍪 Cookies Café · 🇮🇳 India Gate
Hospitality concept sites exploring how far a small menu-driven business can be pushed on the web — motion, photography-led layouts, and static builds that load fast on a phone in a parking lot.

`Next.js` · `Astro` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind`

### 🎞️ axsh.me — my own site
Photography galleries with EXIF captions, a trail log, a journal, and a booking path. Built the way I'd build a client's.

`Next.js 15` · `Framer Motion` · `Three.js` · `Supabase` · `Resend`

## Why I shoot before I design

Composition, lighting and colour are the same problem as layout and visual hierarchy. One just happens to be in a browser.

It means you aren't stitching together a designer, a developer and a photographer who have never spoken to each other. The shot list and the layout get designed at the same time, for each other — which is why my sites don't have stock photos in them.
